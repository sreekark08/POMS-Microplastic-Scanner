Team Ecosphere · Project Brief

Seeing the invisible: rapid microplastic detection in water.

POMS is a low-cost, non-destructive optical scanner. Cross-polarized light makes synthetic polymer fragments glow against a dark field, and a real-time OpenCV pipeline isolates, counts and classifies them frame by frame — no chemicals, no sample destruction, results in seconds.

Working Mechanism

STEP 01

Cross-Polarized Illumination

Two orthogonal polarizing filters block all direct, unscattered light, leaving a near-perfectly dark optical field.
STEP 02

Polymer Birefringence

Synthetic microplastics are optically anisotropic under stress: they rotate the polarized light, so each particle glows brightly against the black background.
STEP 03

OpenCV Computer Vision Pipeline

Each frame is converted to grayscale, Gaussian-blurred, binary-thresholded and contour-filtered by area to isolate, count and classify particles.
